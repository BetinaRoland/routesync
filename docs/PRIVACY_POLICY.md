# RouteSync privacy policy (template)

**This is a starting template. Your college must review and adapt it, and have it checked against the data protection law that applies to you, before publishing.**

## Who we are
[College name] operates RouteSync for college bus transport. Contact: [email] and [phone].

## What we collect
| Data | Whose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, register number, department, year, college email, mobile (optional) | Students | Registration and bus allocation |
| Boarding point chosen or requested | Students | Fee calculation and allocation |
| Estimated annual fee (calculated from distance) | Students | Shown for information; paid at the college office. RouteSync takes no payments. |
| Name, email, phone, assigned bus | Drivers and staff | Operating the service |
| Bus location, speed, heading, timestamps | Buses (from the driver's phone or a GPS device) | Live tracking, arrival times, safety and route review |
| Problem reports and replies | Students | Resolving transport issues |
| Activity log of administrative actions | Staff | Accountability and audit |

## What we do not collect
RouteSync does **not** ask for, collect or track a student's phone location. Arrival estimates use the bus position and the student's fixed boarding point only.

## How long we keep it
Bus location history is deleted after [90] days (configurable). Audit logs are kept for [period required by law]. Other records are kept while the student uses the service, then [period].

## Who can see it
Students see only their own record and their own bus. Drivers see only their own bus, route and rider counts per stop. Route Admins see operational data. Super Admins see everything, including audit logs.

## Services we use
Google Firebase (hosting, authentication, database, notifications), and OpenStreetMap map tiles, place search and routing. These providers process data on our behalf.

## Your choices
You may ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting [email]. Drivers can stop sharing location at any time in the app; the college may have rules requiring it during duty hours.

## Changes
We will update this page when practices change. Last updated: [date].
